Install in two clicks

You need a userscript manager once. Then the script installs like any extension.

  1. Add Violentmonkey to your browser

    It's a free extension that runs small scripts on the sites you choose.

    Other browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, more. Tampermonkey works too.

    Install Violentmonkey

  2. Turn on Allow User Scripts

    Right-click the Violentmonkey icon in your toolbar (pin it from the puzzle icon if you can't see it), choose Manage extension, then switch on Allow User Scripts. You only do this once.

    Prefer a shortcut? Press Open Extension and paste the copied link into a new tab. Browsers don't let web pages open their settings directly, so the last bit is a paste.

    Can't find the switch? On older versions, turn on Developer mode at the top right of the Extensions page instead.

  3. Install the tracker script

    Violentmonkey opens a confirm screen. Press Install there. Updates arrive on their own after that.

    Install script

  4. Open your Zedge dashboard

    The panel appears in the bottom-right corner of upload.zedge.net. If the script page only shows code, finish the steps above first.

    Open Zedge

What it does

Zedge lets you upload 100 items per 28 days. The panel does the counting for you.

Daily allowance
Shows how many you can upload today to stay on pace, about 3 a day. Unused uploads carry over to the next day.
Progress bar
Your 28-day usage at a glance, as used out of 100.
Rolling mode
After your first 28 days it switches to a true rolling window and tells you how many slots free up tomorrow.
Live earnings
Your Zedge revenue since a date you choose. It refreshes every 5 minutes, or click it to refresh now.
History
Every day's count is listed. Fix a mistake or delete an entry whenever you like.
Sri Lanka time
Days roll over at midnight in Asia/Colombo (UTC+5:30).
Light and dark
Tap the sun or moon in the panel header. Your choice is remembered.

How to use it

Logging uploads

  1. On the first run, type how many images you uploaded today (0 if none yet) and press Add. That starts day 1 of your 28 days.
  2. After each upload session, enter the number and press Add.
  3. To log a past day, change the date first.
  4. Watch the big number. That's how many you can upload today.

Setting the earnings date

  1. Open History & settings at the bottom of the panel.
  2. Pick a date under Earnings start date.
  3. The total refreshes right away and your choice is remembered.

Open your business dashboard page on Zedge so the panel can find your business. Until you pick a date it uses 2026-10-18.

Your data stays with you

Everything is stored locally in your browser by your userscript manager. Your upload counts, earnings date and theme never leave your computer.

Backing up

Open the script in your userscript manager, go to its storage view and copy the value of zedgeTracker_v1. Paste it back into the same key to restore.

How earnings work

The dashboard already talks to Zedge's API. The script copies the request settings from the page's own calls and reuses them to ask for your revenue total. So earnings show right after a refresh, the latest settings, including the session token the dashboard sends, are kept in your userscript manager's private storage. They are only ever sent back to Zedge, and are cleared when the session expires. Don't share the zedgeTracker_req value if you copy your data out.
